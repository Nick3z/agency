Discover Nepal

The Land of the Himalayas

Nepal is a landlocked nation nestled between India and China, home to eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including the legendary Mount Everest. A country of extraordinary contrasts — from the scorching Terai plains to the eternal ice of the high Himalayas, from ancient Newari cities to remote Buddhist monasteries.

Over 900 different bird species — a birdwatcher's paradise
UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Pashupatinath, Boudhanath, Swayambhunath & more
Home to 8 of the world's 14 peaks above 8,000 metres
Rich cultural tapestry of 125+ ethnic groups and 123 languages
Birthplace of Lord Buddha in Lumbini
World-class whitewater rafting on Trishuli, Seti & Kali Gandaki rivers

Must-Visit Places

Nepal's Legendary Attractions

From ancient temples to towering peaks, these destinations define the Nepal experience.

Himalaya

Mount Everest Base Camp

Trek to the foot of the world's highest mountain at 5,364m. The 14-day Everest Base Camp trek is Nepal's most iconic journey, passing through Sherpa villages and glacial landscapes.

UNESCO Heritage

Pashupatinath Temple

The holiest Hindu shrine in Nepal, situated on the banks of the Bagmati River in Kathmandu. A sacred complex of temples, ashrams, and cremation ghats that has stood since the 5th century.

UNESCO Heritage

Boudhanath Stupa

One of the world's largest Buddhist stupas, a spiritual hub for the Tibetan diaspora. The all-seeing eyes of Buddha gaze in four directions from this magnificent 36-metre dome.

Lake City

Pokhara & Phewa Lake

Nepal's adventure capital with stunning reflections of the Annapurna range in tranquil Phewa Lake. The gateway to Annapurna treks, paragliding, and caves.

Wildlife

Chitwan National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage jungle hosting Bengal tigers, one-horned rhinos, and gharial crocodiles. Explore by elephant, jeep, or dugout canoe through the tall grass wilderness.

Sacred Site

Lumbini — Birthplace of Buddha

The sacred garden where Siddhartha Gautama was born in 623 BC. A pilgrimage site for millions, with monasteries from 42 countries surrounding the ancient Ashoka Pillar.

Medieval City

Bhaktapur Durbar Square

A medieval city-state frozen in time, with 5th-century pagodas, wood-carved palaces, and potters crafting clay under the watchful eyes of ancient deities.

Trekking

Annapurna Circuit

The world's greatest trek — a 160–230km loop through diverse landscapes from subtropical forests to arctic high passes, crossing the 5,416m Thorong La.

UNESCO Heritage

Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple)

Perched atop a hill with panoramic views of Kathmandu Valley, this 2,500-year-old stupa is draped in prayer flags and frequented by resident monkeys and pilgrims alike.

Things to Do in Nepal

Nepal is far more than trekking. From adrenaline sports to spiritual quests, there's something for every kind of traveler.

🧗

Rock Climbing & Mountaineering

From beginner climbing walls in Nagarjun to technical expeditions on Mera Peak and Island Peak. Nepal has licensed climbing schools and world-class instructors.

All skill levels
🚣

Whitewater Rafting

Nepal's glacial rivers offer Grade 2 to Grade 5 rapids. The Trishuli, Bhote Kosi, and Kali Gandaki rivers are world-renowned for thrilling multi-day raft expeditions.

Mar–Nov season
🪂

Paragliding over Pokhara

Soar above Phewa Lake with the Annapurna range as your backdrop. Pokhara is rated one of the world's top paragliding destinations, with year-round favorable thermals.

Oct–May ideal
🚵

Mountain Biking

Ride ancient trade routes, Himalayan dirt tracks, and technical downhill trails. The Mustang trail and Kathmandu Valley rim ride are legendary circuits.

Guided tours available

About Us

Meet Our Founder

Krishna Man Ranjitkar

Founder & President

Born in the shadow of the Annapurna range in Pokhara, Raj grew up guiding his first trekking groups at age 16. After earning a degree in Tourism Management from Tribhuvan University and a postgraduate diploma from Kathmandu Business School, he founded Nepal Trails International in 2004 with a single mission: to share the real Nepal — not just the postcard version.

Over two decades, Raj has personally guided groups to Everest Base Camp more than 40 times, developed community-based tourism programs in 12 rural villages, and co-authored Nepal's licensed trekking guide curriculum. He is a certified member of the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN), Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA), and the Pacific Asia Travel Association (PATA).
